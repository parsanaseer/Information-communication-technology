Eight Uses if ICT in School Life:

A. ICT for Learning:

>> Online Learning:
- Use:  Students learn through online classes and educational videos.
- Advantage:  Students can learn from anywhere.
- Disadvantage:  Internet connection is required.

>>Digital Books:
- Use:   Students read books and notes on computers.
- Advantage:   Easy access to many books.
- Disadvantage:   Long screen time can cause eye strain.

B. ICT for Communication:

 >>Email:
- Use:  Teachers send notices and homework through email.
- Advantage:  Messages are delivered quickly.
- Disadvantage:  Important messages can get lost in spam.

>>Video Conferencing:
- Use:     Teachers and students communicate through video calls.
- Advantage:     Face-to-face communication from different places.
- Disadvantage:    Poor internet can interrupt calls.

C. ICT for Management:

>> Student Attendance System:
- Use:     Schools record student attendance digitally.
- Advantage:     Saves time and keeps accurate records.
- Disadvantage:    Technical problems can affect records.

>>Computerized Result System:
- Use:  Schools prepare student results using computers.
- Advantage:  Results can be calculated quickly.
- Disadvantage:  Incorrect data can produce wrong results.

D. ICT for Entertainment:

>> Educational Games:
- Use: Students play educational games for learning.

>>Watching Educational Videos:
- Use:   Students watch educational and entertaining videos.
- Advantage:   Makes difficult topics easier to understand.
- Disadvantage:  Students may become distracted by unnecessary videos.
